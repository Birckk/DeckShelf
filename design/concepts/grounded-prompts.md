# Grounded collection concepts — 28 September 2026

Tool: built-in image generation, not Ming. Actual local Giada, Lathliss, Ghalta and Sai card images supplied as references. These are exploratory design mockups; card text, mana identity and other generated details are not authoritative. The working application has not been modified.

## E — Card studio

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a refined, practical desktop UI screenshot for Commander Archive, a personal Magic: The Gathering commander deck showcase. Landscape 16:10. Four attached reference images are the actual commander cards: Giada, Lathliss, Ghalta, Sai. Use these real card faces intact, with their complete borders, mana cost, title, illustration and rules boxes. Preserve their proportions and art. The product is a showcase, not a card editor or shop. The user finds the existing forest-green and gold app dull, disliked excessive cosmic 3D mockups, and wants the actual cards to be the main attraction. No space scenes, no stars, no floating boxes, no helix in this mockup, no particle effects, no large marketing heading, no welcome banner, no permanent sidebar. The app already has a beloved animated random picker which is a separate tab and not to be redesigned here. Show a slim modern UI header: small "Commander Archive" wordmark, compact view tabs "Collection", "Cards", "Pick a deck", with an elegant dice-like icon on Pick a deck; small "My collection" at far right. Fine typography similar to Outfit with DM Sans, strong readable contrast, subtle tactile button design. No invented official Magic logo or avatar, no counters/statistics dashboard. The screen must look buildable and attractive, use visual hierarchy and beautiful spacing rather than decoration. Main deck view occupies almost all the app. Compact search, the five authentic Magic mana symbols, "Exact colors" toggle, and a "Filters" popover button are easy to locate without competing with cards.
DIRECTION: CARD STUDIO. Rich neutral charcoal-black background (not green), with an extremely soft broad warm gray illumination behind the cards, inspired by a physical photographic lightbox. An uncomplicated horizontal carousel, precisely one row, straight horizontal center line. Five to seven complete portrait Magic cards visible across the width, center Giada card dominant about 430px tall in a 900px viewport, immediate left and right Lathliss and Sai about 360px tall, Ghalta plus repeated cards small at far edges with slight cropping by viewport. All faces remain frontal and flat, no twisting or rotation. Very slight overlap of far cards, smooth size falloff and restrained grounding shadows suggest interactive sliding. Every carousel item is ONLY the actual card image with no custom labels, captions, icons or panels pasted over it. No extra frame surrounding the cards, no custom fake card face. Keep all needed controls separate. Beneath center card a short clean two-line selected-deck label "Calling All Angels" and small "Giada, Font of Hope", with a discreet external-link icon on right; this metadata is in the empty page area, not attached to or overlaying the card. Small round previous/next arrows bracket a "01 / 29" indicator below this label. Compact search and filters are a single slim strip under the header. A quiet warm-white accent highlights the Cards tab and controls; rich color comes from the actual cards. The carousel is large, with balanced margins and no huge dead header area. Showcase subtle realistic reflected light along existing card edges that a CSS or Three.js implementation could recreate, never a neon glow. A small practical bottom-right "Pick a deck" button links to existing random picker.

DIRECTION: COLLECTION CATALOGUE. A clean warm light-gray/off-white editorial app with dark graphite text, slightly warm white card wells, muted ink-blue active controls. A beautifully arranged 5-column grid, each tile about 250px wide, two rows with lower row partly beyond the viewport. Display the real FULL portrait commander card, with all printed rules and borders visible, inside a tiny neutral padded tile area. No cropping into art-only thumbnails. Below each complete card a separate clean compact metadata area, with deck name in 17px medium sans ("Calling All Angels", "Reign of Dragons", "Tramplesaurus Rex", "Keen Engineering"), small set label "Foundations", and a clearly aligned row of mana icons at lower right. Distinct fixed places for title, set and icons on every tile. No text or badges overlay the actual card. Use the attached four card images; repeat them where necessary to make the grid composition without inventing card art. One hovered tile is lifted only 4px with a slight smooth shadow and a discreet external-link icon in its metadata area, not a giant button across the art. Header and search/filter toolbar together take less than 110px. Search at left, five mana filter buttons in middle, "Exact colors", Filters, sort icon on right. The Collection tab is active. Tiny "29 decks" label integrated in toolbar, no standalone large page title. A slim dark ink "Pick a deck" action sits in header. The page should feel like a meticulously typeset collectors' catalogue and modern creative tool, with color provided by the full card artworks and visual energy from disciplined composition; no parchment, no ornate fantasy border, no faux wood shelves, no sticker UI.
